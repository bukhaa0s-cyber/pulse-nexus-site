Support

Questions, a bug or a purchase problem? Email bukhaa0s@gmail.com and include your device model. We reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

  • I changed phones. If you signed in, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and your progress and gems come back. Guest progress stays on the old phone, so sign in (Settings → Account) before you switch.
  • A purchase didn't arrive. Make sure you are signed in to the same account, then reopen the game. If it is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Delete my account. Settings → Delete account. It deletes your account and save immediately. See the privacy policy.

  • حذف حسابي. الإعدادات ← حذف الحساب، ويُحذف حسابك وحفظك فوراً. التفاصيل في سياسة الخصوصية.
